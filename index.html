Yu Liu is currently a postgraduate student in the School of Information Science and Engineering at Shandong Normal University.

Yu Liu's research lies in the areas of multimodal sentiment analysis. Multimodal emotion analysis mainly has the following research branches. The first is modal feature extraction, which extracts emotion-related features from different modal data such as text, image and audio. The second is modal fusion, which combines different modal features through early-stage, late-stage or hybrid fusion. The third is emotion classification and regression, which classifies emotions or predicts their intensity. The fourth is cross-domain and cross-cultural emotion analysis, which addresses the differences in emotion analysis in different domains and cultural backgrounds. The fifth is interpretability research, which aims to explain the basis and process of the model's emotion judgment to improve the credibility of the model.

Project

A Classroom Quality Evaluation System Based on Multimodal Sentiment Analysis

Project Dates: April 2025 - present

Research Areas: multimodal sentiment analysis; facial expression recognition

Project Description: The classroom quality evaluation system focuses on the digital upgrade of the teaching scenario and comprehensively applies multimodal information processing technologies to achieve intelligent assessment of classroom teaching quality. By analyzing the class videos captured by the cameras in the classroom, the system uses advanced facial expression recognition algorithms to accurately capture students' microexpressions such as smiles and frowns. Combined with the emotion analysis model, it deeply analyzes students' emotional states in the classroom and judges their learning enthusiasm. Through intelligent video capture and posture detection algorithms, it calculates the head-up rate in the classroom in real time and analyzes students' concentration and participation. Based on a large dataset labeled with the states of students in class, it trains and optimizes the analysis algorithms to achieve accurate identification of students' listening states, covering various states such as concentration, confusion, and fatigue. This system provides data support for teachers to adjust teaching strategies and for the school to evaluate teaching quality, and helps to promote the development of education and teaching towards intelligence and precision.
